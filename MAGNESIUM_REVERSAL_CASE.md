# Clinical evidence reversal: magnesium after myocardial infarction

Archival `metadat::dat.egger2001` has 16 trials. Peto OR before ISIS-4 (15 records): **0.55** (95% CI 0.44–0.68); after ISIS-4: **1.01** (0.95–1.07). Source: https://wviechtb.github.io/metadat/reference/dat.egger2001.html

Challenge: distinguish an odds ratio from a risk ratio, investigate timing/co-treatment differences, and avoid treating this contrast as proof of publication bias. Run `Rscript scripts/verify_magnesium_reversal.R` to check a *selected six-record teaching subset*, not the full review.
