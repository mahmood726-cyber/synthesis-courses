How to use this starter

Make a real methodological decision, inspect immediate feedback, and continue. You can revise wrong answers, but the first attempt stays recorded locally so your practice recommendations remain meaningful. The path is untimed; allow around 10–15 minutes. You can also review any earlier decision.

The real sources behind your decisions
