04 / Reproduce an independent second real dataset in AllMeta

Your statistical laboratory — real six-study lidocaine data.

The Hine et al. archival data (metadat::dat.hine1989) contain genuine event counts. Select two to six studies and calculate the treatment-minus-control risk difference (null = 0) with a common-effect inverse-variance teaching estimator. This is not the magnesium Peto odds ratio or a clinical recommendation.

Input scale: RD on identity scale; choose label “Risk difference” and null 0 in the Workbench or Forest Plot Viewer. Other estimators and variance conventions may yield different pooled results. Compare the methods explicitly.

Privacy/compatibility: direct staging works only when the course and AllMeta share the same GitHub Pages origin. It checks for existing bus data and requires your explicit approval before replacing anything. Otherwise use CSV. Synthesis cannot certify the result displayed by an external app.
