Two-Person Review Exchange

Work independently first. Then compare, reconcile and document disagreements. This is a human-operated, no-account exercise—not simulated agreement between two AI personas.

Scope: Six real trial-arm records from metadat::dat.egger2001, selected from 16 historical studies. Counts are genuine archival teaching records, but eligibility cannot be confirmed without primary publications. Reviewer names are self-declared; the app cannot establish that two distinct people took part.

How a genuinely independent session works

  1. Send Reviewer A and Reviewer B the link and the same prespecified screening question. Each person uses their own device/browser and completes the form privately.
  2. Each reviewer exports a JSON decision pack, then sends it to a human adjudicator by their chosen communication channel. Avoid sharing one person's decisions before the other finishes.
  3. The adjudicator imports both completed packs together; the page reveals agreements/disagreements and allows final decisions with documented reasons. Export a local Markdown report.

The form contains no patient data and makes no network uploads. The files contain human-authored decisions and labels: treat them as research working documents. JSON is not signed, reviewers are not authenticated, and an independence declaration is not proof of independence.
