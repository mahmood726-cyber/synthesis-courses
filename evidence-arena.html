Facilitator use: a reproducible case conference

Ask one learner to defend the precommitted selection rule, another to reconstruct the effect, and a third to challenge the clinical interpretation. Participants submit their own dossier. The source data are real; the peer-review exercise and intervention sequence are instructional, not archived conversations.

For independent R reproductions use magnesium, BCG and lidocaine. Each source's study counts and methods must be checked against the appropriate R package. Peer review, accessibility testing with assistive technology, learning-outcome evaluation and professional translation reviews remain necessary.
